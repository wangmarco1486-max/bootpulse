Zero-Jargon Financial Academy

Core concepts every founder needs.

Burn Rate

Gross Burn = total money leaving the bank each month.
Net Burn = Gross Expenses − Revenue. Negative Net Burn = profit.

Runway

Months until cash reaches zero.
Runway = Cash ÷ Net Monthly Burn.
